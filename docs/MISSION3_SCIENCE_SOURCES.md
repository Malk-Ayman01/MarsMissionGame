# Mission 3 – Science check & sources

Every fact shown to players in Mission 3, what is real, and what is game story.

| In the game | Real? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A rover must plan its power (Heater #2 wastes power, 400 W limit) | **Game story** based on a real idea: Perseverance's power source makes about 110 W, batteries cover peaks up to 900 W | NASA JPL – Perseverance power system: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/launch/mission/spacecraft/power/ |
| Dust devils are weak and can't flip the rover; dust costs a little battery | **Real**: Mars air is very thin; dust devils even cleaned Spirit's solar panels | NASA/JPL Spirit "cleaning events" (2005) |
| Two-stage rocket; Stage 1 is dropped to make the rocket lighter | **Real design** (NASA Mars Ascent Vehicle, two solid rocket motors) | NASA NTRS 20205008210: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20205008210 |
| Target orbit 343 km above Mars | **Real design value** (MAV DAC-0.0) | same NTRS paper |
| Orbit speed ≈ 3,400 m/s | **Calculated** from Mars' gravity for a 343 km circular orbit | physics (v = √(GM/r)) |
| The rocket must steer itself; radio takes about 3–22 min each way | **Real** (distance 55–401 million km ÷ speed of light) | ESA Mars Express blog; NASA DSN |
| People ride the rocket and dock with a spaceship | **Game story**. Real plan: robots launch the sample tubes, an orbiter catches them. NASA's Mars Sample Return lost its funding in 2026 | NASA MSR page; news coverage, Jan 2026 |
| Astronauts exercise ~2 h/day; bone loss ~1%/month | **Real** (Space Station) | NASA / CSA astronaut health pages |
| Day-and-night lights help sleep | **Real** (Space Station LED lighting) | NASA lighting research |
| Astronauts grew and ate lettuce in space | **Real** (NASA Veggie on the Space Station) | NASA Veggie |

Choices removed because they had no real science behind them:
- Power console "helper tool" (Ray & Dust Shield / Rock Scanner / Battery Boost)
- Rocket setup (Heavy Hull / Light Auto-Pilot / Science Pod)
- Final "home ship boost" after docking
